License

Payment and license recovery

Premium isn't for sale yet. Here's how activation will work, and how to recover a license once it is.

How it will work

A Premium license is a small signed file, verified entirely offline — Sindal contacts no server to validate it and creates no account. Once purchased, it's pasted into the Offer tab of the app to unlock export. The purchase is one-time and covers every update within the current major version.

I lost my license key — how do I recover it?

Once Premium is open for sale, email {{email}} with your proof of purchase (the confirmation email you received when ordering). Keep that email: it's the only record of your purchase, since Sindal stores no account data server-side.

Do I have to pay again for every update?

No. The purchase covers every update within the current major version. A major-version change, if it happens, will be announced on this page ahead of time.

How do I activate Premium once it's available?

Paste the key you receive by email into the field on the Offer tab, inside the app. No connection is needed to activate it.

Questions about a future order, or need to recover a key? Email {{email}}.
