License

How it will work

A Premium license is a small signed file, verified entirely offline. No account, no subscription, no server contacted. You paste the key you receive by email into the app's Offer tab, and export unlocks.

Do I pay again for every update?

No, the purchase covers all 1.x versions. If a version 2 ever ships, it will be announced here ahead of time.

I lost my key, how do I get it back?

Email sindal@gmail.fr with your purchase confirmation. Keep it safe: Sindal stores no accounts, so it is the only record of your purchase.
