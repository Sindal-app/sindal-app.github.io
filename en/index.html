How it works

Four moves, from settings to file

Set

Ten moods (happy, sad, epic, celtic, oriental, classical…), twelve keys, the tempo, the length, and three metres: 4/4, a waltz in 3/4 or a lilting 6/8. To go further, about twenty sliders: chords, rhythm, rests, dynamics, refrain. Or let the guided mode ask you a few questions.

Generate

Melody, left hand and bass are written together. No wrong notes: everything stays in the scale, and the piece ends on a real cadence.

Listen, then rework

Select the bars that miss: three variations to hear in context, and you keep the best. You can also add, remove or copy bars, or open the note editor. An existing MIDI file can serve as a starting point (Premium).

Save

MIDI, WAV, a PNG score, MusicXML, and a .sindal project that reopens the piece exactly as you left it. What is free, what is not.

Sound

A bank included, yours on top

A MIDI file describes notes, not sounds: something has to play them. Sindal brings its own instrument, so it sounds right away with nothing to set up.

🎛️

GeneralUser GS included

S. Christian Collins' General MIDI SoundFont: 128 instruments, 32 MB. Its licence allows musical use, commercial included.

📚

Your own banks

Load a .sf2 SoundFont or an .sfz bank, a sampled piano for instance. It is remembered between launches.

💾

The WAV sounds like the app

Export is rendered by the active bank: what you hear is what you get.

An .sfz bank holds a single instrument, so the instrument picker greys out. That is expected: the bank is the instrument.

Help

Frequently asked questions

The sound is poor, how can I improve it?

The timbre comes from the active bank. The bundled one is all-round; a better-sampled bank sounds noticeably more real.

  1. Download a .sf2 SoundFont or an .sfz bank (a sampled piano, for example).
  2. In Settings, choose Browse for a .sf2 or .sfz bank.
  3. Listen again. The exported WAV will use the same one.

To go back to the original sound: Restore the default bank.

How do I change instrument?

In Settings, the Instrument list. The piece isn't recomposed, only the timbre changes. The list is greyed out with an .sfz bank; load a .sf2 to get the choice back.

I hear nothing: "No audio output"

No bank is loaded. Load a .sf2 or .sfz from Settings, or use Restore the default bank. Also check your system's audio output and volume.

"Instrument missing from this SoundFont — silent"

The loaded SoundFont doesn't have the chosen instrument. Pick another in the list, or go back to the default bank, which covers all 128 General MIDI instruments.

My .sfz bank produces no sound

The file is read, but its samples (.wav, .flac) can't be found. Keep them in the bank's folder with the original layout: absolute paths, or paths leading out of the folder, are refused for safety.

My export has no WAV file

Audio export is part of the Premium offer. If you have it, check that a bank is loaded: without one, no WAV is written.

Why does my piece stop at 12 bars?

That is the free offer's limit. Beyond it, generation points you to Premium. See the offers.

Can the bass use a different instrument?

Yes, with Premium: the Bass bank checkbox in Settings.
