    Requirements

    What you need

    WindowsWindows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Nothing else to install.
    macOSApple Silicon (M1 and later). No build for Intel Macs.
    Disk spaceAbout 250 MB, including 32 MB of sound bank.
    ConnectionNone. Only the update check, on Windows, uses the network.
    SoundAny sound card will do: synthesis is built in, no external synthesiser needed.
